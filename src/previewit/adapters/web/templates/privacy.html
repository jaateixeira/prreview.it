Outside the European Economic Area

Some AI services and Paddle work outside the EEA, for example in the United States. Data goes there under an adequacy decision of the European Commission (such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework) or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses. When you choose a model that runs in a country without either, such as China, your work goes there only because you chose it and asked for that review.

How long we keep it

  • Your account: until you ask us to delete it.
  • Work, reviews and AI reviews: as long as the review space they belong to keeps them, or until your account is deleted.
  • A form filled in before signing in: one hour. A request prepared by an AI assistant: seven days.
  • Purchases and credit history: as long as Finnish accounting law requires, up to ten years.
  • Server logs: at most 90 days.

Your rights

You have the right to access your data, have it corrected, have it erased, restrict its use, receive it in a portable form, object to its use, and withdraw any consent you gave. Write to the privacy contact above; we answer within one month. You may also complain to a supervisory authority; in Finland it is the Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuojavaltuutettu, tietosuoja.fi).

Security

Previewit is served over HTTPS only. Passwords are stored as Argon2 hashes, never as they were typed. Keys you give for AI models are never stored. Access to the servers and the database is limited to the people who run them.

Cookies

Previewit sets only the cookies it needs to work, so it asks for no consent:

  • pv_session: keeps you signed in, for up to 14 days.
  • pv_csrf: protects the forms you send from other sites.
  • pv_flow: for ten minutes while you sign in with Google, Apple or HAKA.

When you open Paddle's checkout to buy credit, Paddle sets its own cookies under its privacy notice. Previewit has no tracking or advertising cookies.
